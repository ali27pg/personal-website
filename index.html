About

“My work starts from consent and respect — the camera should give dignity, never take it.”

Md Ali Akbar is a final-year Bachelor of Media & Communication student at Albukhary International University, Malaysia — studying as a fully funded Albukhary Foundation Scholar with a CGPA of 3.85, and working toward an academic career in media and communication research.

His direction is academia, but he doesn't lock himself to a single path. Journalism, filmmaking, public relations, research — he moves between them because the work demands it, and because he can. His strongest motivation is a dream he refuses to postpone: he doesn't wait until he reaches the final destination to begin; he starts walking now, with whatever he has.

What carries him forward is a God-given gift: he learns quickly, understands things deeply, and adapts to new fields without losing much time. That is how a madrasah student from Bangladesh became a photographer, filmmaker, podcaster, student leader, and researcher — all before graduating.

Beyond the work — travelling · football · activism on social issues
