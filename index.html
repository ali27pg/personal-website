About

“My work starts from consent and respect — the camera should give dignity, never take it.”

Md Ali Akbar is a Media & Communication undergraduate at Albukhary International University, Malaysia, where he studies as a fully funded Albukhary Foundation Scholar with a CGPA of 3.80.

He is a documentary photographer and media producer whose practice centers on dignity-first visual storytelling — most notably Far From Home, an in-progress photo essay on Bangladeshi migrant workers in Malaysia, built on consent and years of conversations across the country.

His academic interests span public relations, semiotics, journalistic writing, and news framing, and he is building a research profile aimed at fully funded postgraduate study. He has led student-community operations as General Secretary of the Bangladeshi Students Community at AIU — organizing 40+ programs — and now serves as its Crisis Management Adviser.
